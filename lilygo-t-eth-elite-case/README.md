## Printing / ordering

The author printed it on an MK3S+ at **0.2 mm layers with no supports**, and
notes that the **buttons have to be cut free** after printing.

For JLC3DP, order it in **MJF PA12-HP Nylon**:

* the button tabs have to flex — resin snaps them;
* it sits on a PoE board that runs warm, and PA12's heat deflection is 175 °C
  against roughly 50–60 °C for SLA resin, which also yellows under UV;
* no supports, so no witness marks in the port openings;
* ±0.3 mm tolerance and 1.0 mm minimum wall are both comfortably met.

One caveat: the thin bridges holding the buttons may simply break off during
depowdering. That is where a knife was going anyway, but the loose buttons can
get lost in the powder — worth ordering two sets.
